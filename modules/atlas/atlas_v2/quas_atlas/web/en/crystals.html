Crystals: lattice and cell

A crystal is one and the same cell repeated in three directions. Six numbers — the edge lengths a, b, c and the angles α, β, γ — together with the contents of the cell fix everything: the density, the distances between planes of atoms and the angles at which the crystal reflects X-rays. A solid-state calculation starts from the cell, and so does the work of Compass with CIF files.

What to understand

  • The cell volume V = abc·√(1 − cos²α − cos²β − cos²γ + 2cosα·cosβ·cosγ); for a cube a³, for a hexagonal cell a²c·√3/2.
  • The density ρ = Z·M/(N_A·V) with Z formula units in the cell; it is used to check a solved structure.
  • The planes (hkl) are d apart (for a cube d = a/√(h² + k² + l²)) and reflect rays at the Bragg angle: λ = 2d·sin θ.
  • Centred lattices extinguish whole series of reflections: I keeps h + k + l even, F keeps h, k, l of one parity; the extinctions tell the lattice type.
Model limits: a perfect rigid lattice, line positions without intensities
What is computed
Volume and density from the cell parameters; interplanar distances, Bragg angles and multiplicities of the allowed reflections; atoms per cell, coordination and space filling of cubic packings.
Which approximations are made
A lattice without defects and thermal motion; intensities are not computed (a line’s height is its multiplicity); extinctions come only from the centring (and the diamond and hcp bases), without the screw axes and glide planes of other groups.
What can be concluded
How the cell fixes the density and the line positions of a pattern and how the extinctions reveal the lattice — yes; full intensities need the structure factor, and energies a periodic quantum-chemical calculation.

Choose NaCl: a = 5.640 Å, Z = 4. What is the density?

ρ, g/cm³

2.16 g/cm³: V = 5.640³ = 179.4 Å³, ρ = 4·58.44/(6.022·10²³·179.4·10⁻²⁴) = 2.16 — as in the tables (2.165).

Copper (fcc, a = 3.615 Å), Cu Kα radiation. Where is the first line of the pattern?

2θ, °

43.3° — the (111) reflection: in fcc (100) and (110) are extinct (indices of mixed parity), the first allowed plane is (111), d = 3.615/√3 = 2.087 Å.

Silicon (diamond lattice): is there a (200) line in the pattern?

No: for diamond with all-even h, k, l one also needs h + k + l = 4n, and (200) sums to 2. So (220) follows (111) directly.

What fraction of the volume do the spheres fill in a face-centred cubic packing?

Filling

74 % = π/(3√2): the closest packing, like the hexagonal one. bcc fills 68 %, simple cubic 52 %, diamond 34 %.
