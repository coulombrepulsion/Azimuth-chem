What to understand

  • A point group is the set of all symmetry operations of a molecule; it is found from the symmetry elements (Schoenflies scheme).
  • Conjugate operations form a class; there are as many irreducible representations as classes.
  • Characters of irreducible representations show how orbitals, vibrations and the functions x, y, z behave under the group operations.
  • The reduction formula answers “how many and of which kind”: vibrations, SALCs, sublevels in a splitting.
  • The characters of the representation spanned by the ligand orbitals are the numbers of ligands left in place; the reduction formula splits it into irreducible ones, and the projection operator gives the SALCs.

Descent in symmetry

An operation remains in the group only if the modified molecule coincides with itself after it. Therefore the new group is always a subgroup of the original one, and each of its classes lies entirely within one of the old classes.

Check your knowledge

Exercises on the page: do the action on the page and choose an answer — it is checked right away.

Open Oh, the “Classes” tab. Why are 3C₂ (= C₄²) and 6C₂ different classes if they are the same 180° rotations?

3C₂ are rotations about the coordinate axes (the same ones along which the C₄ lie), while 6C₂ are rotations about axes through the midpoints of opposite edges. No operation of the group takes an axis of the first type into one of the second type, so the rotations are not conjugate and form different classes.

Oh → “elongation along z”. What does the class 3C₂ become?

Into C₂ (rotation about z) and 2C₂′ (about x and y). After elongation the z axis is no longer equivalent to x and y, and the former class splits in two.

Td (CH₄), the “Γ counting” tab. How many bands will the IR spectrum show, and how many lines the Raman spectrum?

Γvib = A₁ + E + 2T₂. Only T₂ (like x, y, z) are IR active — 2 bands. A₁, E and both T₂ are Raman active — 4 lines.

C3v, the “Multiplication” tab. Compare the cells C₃·σv and σv·C₃. What do they show?

Both products are reflections, but in different planes: C₃ and σv do not commute, so the group C3v is non-Abelian. The order matters: in A·B, B is performed first.

D4h ([PtCl₄]²⁻), the “Character table”. Which d orbital belongs to b1g and which to b2g, and what does this depend on?

dx²−y² is b1g, dxy is b2g. This follows from the convention that the C₂′ axes pass through the ligands, i.e., along x and y. If the axes are rotated by 45°, the labels swap.

“Reducible representation and SALCs”: into which irreducible representations do the hydrogen 1s orbitals of CH₄ decompose?

A₁ + T₂: Γ = (4, 1, 0, 0, 2); the reduction formula gives n(A₁) = (4 + 8 + 12)/24 = 1, n(T₂) = (12 + 12)/24 = 1. They bond with the 2s (A₁) and the three 2p (T₂) of carbon.
